AquaPulse Privacy Policy

100% On-Device Zero-Knowledge Architecture
← Return to App

1. Zero Data Collection Guarantee

AquaPulse Water Intake Tracker is engineered under a strict 100% Local-First, Zero-Knowledge Privacy Architecture. We do not operate any backend servers, analytics trackers, telemetry scripts, or advertising SDKs.

2. Where Your Data Lives

All hydration logs, timestamps, notes, and daily goal preferences are stored exclusively on your own device using your browser or Android WebView's local IndexedDB and localStorage containers.

3. Data Portability & Deletion

You retain complete ownership and control over your records at all times. You can export your data to a local JSON file (`Export JSON`) or permanently erase all stored records (`Clear All`) directly within the application with immediate effect.
